OUR HISTORY

Liverpool FC has a rich legacy, with numerous championship titles over the years. Being part of Liverpool Football Club means being part of the greatest football family in the world. We look out for each other, and particularly those who are in need.

Our Vision

At Liverpool FC, we strive for greatness on and off the pitch. Our vision is to remain a globally competitive team while continuing to embody the values of sportsmanship, community, and unity with our fans.
