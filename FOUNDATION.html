About the LFC Foundation

The LFC Foundation is the official charity of Liverpool Football Club. Our mission is to deliver sustainable and long-term positive change to the lives of children, young people, and families across the Liverpool City Region and beyond.

We work with local communities and global partners to provide support in four key areas:

  • Mental Health and Wellbeing
  • Youth and Educational Empowerment
  • Diversity and Inclusion
  • Sports Engagement and Employability

With our programmes, we aim to empower individuals by providing resources and support for personal and professional development. Whether it’s offering scholarships, running community camps, or promoting physical and mental health through sports, we are committed to making a difference.
